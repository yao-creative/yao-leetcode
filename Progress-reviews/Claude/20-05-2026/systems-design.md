A few things worth calling out:

**Table 1** is your honest gap map — green means you're fine, amber means you have surface coverage but would get exposed on a harder variant, red means you'd likely blank on it mid-interview.

**Table 2** is cumulative — each tier assumes you've absorbed everything below it. The quant tier in particular is less about specific LeetCode problems and more about mathematical reasoning speed and knowing obscure-but-elegant patterns cold (reservoir sampling, modular arithmetic, divide-and-conquer variants).

**Table 3** is where most people are caught off-guard. Frontier AI firms increasingly ask ML-adjacent system design (inference serving, vector search, feature stores) — not just the classic URL shortener. HFT firms want you to know *why* a std::unordered_map is unacceptable on a hot path and what you'd use instead.

Your biggest leverage right now: **graphs + DP depth + system design foundations**. Those three unlock the startup tier quickly and start opening the frontier AI door.
